## Calculate Korea's 2026 weekly holiday pay
Korea's paid weekly holiday allowance can be confusing for hourly and part-time workers. Enter the wage and work pattern to review **eligibility, paid holiday hours, and weekly/monthly estimates** together.

## Key features
- **Eligibility checks** for 4-week average 15+ hours, absence, and a continuing 7-day week
- **Short-hours estimate** using weekly hours ÷ comparable full-time workdays
- **Eight-hour daily cap and Korea's 2026 minimum-wage example**
- **Weekly and monthly estimate** in one screen
- **Example, copy, clear, and field-specific error feedback**

## How to use
1. Enter the hourly wage in KRW.
2. Enter the scheduled weekly hours averaged over four weeks.
3. Confirm the comparable regular worker's weekly workdays (usually five).
4. Mark whether there was an absence on a scheduled day and whether employment continued through the full seven-day week.

The result stays blank until the required values are valid. Invalid, fractional, or out-of-range entries show a field-specific message, and Copy result remains disabled until a valid estimate is available.

## Example
- Hourly wage: 10,320 KRW (Korea's 2026 minimum wage)
- Weekly hours: 20
- Comparable regular worker's workdays: 5
- No absence and full-week employment: Yes

→ Paid holiday hours: 4h, weekly holiday pay: about 41,280 KRW

## Notes
- This is a simplified estimate under Korean labor rules, not legal advice.
- The 15-hour test generally uses 4-week average scheduled hours, not merely hours actually worked.
- Irregular schedules or a workplace without a clear comparable regular worker may require a different calculation.
- Final payroll is determined by labor contract, attendance logs, and company policy.
- Overtime/night/holiday premiums are outside this calculator’s scope.

Review the legal basis at the [Korean Law Information Center's Enforcement Decree of the Labor Standards Act, Article 30](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?chrClsCd=010202&lspttninfSeq=148916), and check the wage example against the [Ministry of Employment and Labor's 2026 minimum-wage notice](https://www.moel.go.kr/mainpop2.do).
