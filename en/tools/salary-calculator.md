## Check your 2026 take-home pay before negotiating salary
A higher gross salary may produce a smaller cash-flow increase than expected because income tax and Korea's four major social insurance programs are deducted. This calculator converts annual gross salary into an estimated monthly take-home amount for job changes, salary negotiations, and household budgeting.

## Key features
- Monthly and yearly take-home pay estimates
- Separate monthly income and local income tax estimate
- Employee pension, health, long-term care, and employment insurance deductions
- Optional monthly non-taxable pay
- Simplified adjustments for dependents and children

## How to use the calculator
### 1. Enter annual gross salary
Example: `50,000,000` KRW

### 2. Enter monthly non-taxable pay, if any
Example: `200,000` KRW for eligible recurring items such as a meal allowance.

### 3. Enter dependents and children
Dependents exclude you but must include eligible children. For a spouse and one child, enter 2 dependents and 1 child.

## Example
- Annual gross salary: KRW 50 million
- Monthly non-taxable pay: KRW 200,000
- Dependents excluding yourself: 0
- Children: 0

Results update immediately with estimated monthly and yearly take-home pay, monthly tax, and monthly employee social insurance.

## 2026 calculation basis
- Employee pension: 4.75% of monthly taxable pay, capped at a KRW 6.59 million monthly base
- Employee health insurance: 3.595% of monthly taxable pay
- Long-term care: health premium multiplied by `0.9448% / 7.19%`
- Employee employment insurance: 0.9% of monthly taxable pay
- Income and local tax: 6–45% progressive rates after simplified earned-income, personal, and insurance deductions, plus 10% local income tax
- Children must be included in the dependent count; a simplified child tax credit is also applied

> This is not an exact reproduction of Korea's official monthly payroll withholding table. Actual withholding can differ due to employer allowances, dependent eligibility, the selected withholding percentage, and year-end tax settlement.

## Input and empty-state guidance
Enter whole KRW amounts. The calculator explains missing, negative, fractional, oversized, or inconsistent values instead of silently correcting them. Monthly non-taxable pay cannot exceed monthly gross pay, and the children count cannot exceed total dependents. Use **Try example** for a sample calculation or **Clear all** to return to the empty state.

## FAQ
### Why can the result differ from my payslip?
Allowances, tax-exempt items, mid-year employment changes, payroll withholding choices, and year-end settlement vary by employer and employee.

### Can freelancers or business owners use this calculator?
This calculator is designed for Korean employment income. Business and freelance income require a different tax calculation.

### How should I enter a bonus?
You may add a fixed annual bonus to gross annual salary for a rough estimate. Variable bonuses can produce a larger difference from actual payroll.

### Why is calculation blocked when children exceed dependents?
Eligible children must also be part of the dependent count. For example, with one spouse and one child, enter 2 dependents and 1 child.
