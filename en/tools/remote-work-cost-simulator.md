## Why compare remote work and commuting costs?
Work-style decisions often feel emotional, but a lot of the difference comes from repeated daily friction:
- transport
- lunch and coffee
- extra home-office spending
- commute time every month

This tool turns those tradeoffs into a quick monthly comparison so you can see whether office days or remote days feel more expensive under your own routine.

## What it compares
Enter:
- total workdays per month
- office days per month
- round-trip commute cost per office day
- lunch and coffee/snack spend per office day
- extra at-home cost per remote day
- daily round-trip commute time
- optional hourly value for your time

The calculator shows your current mixed cash cost, monthly commute time, and equal-workday full-office versus full-remote scenarios. When you enter an hourly value, the scenario gap also includes the commute-time value of a full-office month.

## How to use the calculator
1. Enter your total workdays and current office days for the month.
2. Add round-trip transport, lunch, and coffee or snack costs for one office day.
3. Enter the extra utility, food, or workspace cost for one remote day.
4. Add your round-trip commute in minutes.
5. Optionally enter an hourly value for your time.
6. Review your current mixed cash cost alongside the full-office and full-remote monthly scenarios.

Use the preset buttons to switch quickly between full remote, roughly two office days per week, and full office. If an entry is blank, outside the allowed range, or office days exceed total workdays, the result area explains what needs to be corrected.

## Monthly calculation rules
- Current mixed cash cost = (office-day cost × office days) + (extra remote-day cost × remote days).
- Full-office cash cost = office-day cost × monthly workdays.
- Full-remote cash cost = extra remote-day cost × monthly workdays.
- Salary, employer subsidies, remote-work allowances, and one-time equipment purchases are not added automatically. Enter only your actual personal daily costs.

## Useful situations

## FAQ
### Are the results exact financial advice?
No. They are a planning estimate. Employer meal or transport support, transit passes, remote-work allowances, equipment purchases, and personal habits can change your real costs.

### What should I use as the hourly value of time?
You can use your take-home hourly pay, a freelance rate, or a consistent personal estimate of what an hour is worth. Consistency is more useful here than accounting precision.

### What belongs in the extra remote-day cost?
Include only costs that increase because you work from home, such as electricity, heating or cooling, home coffee and snacks, coworking fees, or equipment wear.
