## Why this move checklist planner helps
Moving is not just a busy day. It is a schedule that can unravel when bookings, address changes, refrigerator cleanup, or the first-night box are left too late.

This planner turns your move date and setup into an ordered checklist for **two weeks before, three days before, and move day**, with the actual calendar date shown for every stage.

Each section includes its actual calendar date. Empty, past, invalid, and more-than-five-years-away dates are rejected with a clear message.

## How to build your moving checklist
1. Choose your move date.
2. Select the home or workspace type.
3. Choose full-service, partial/self packing, or a self move.
4. Set the elevator access condition.
5. Check whether utility and internet transfers are needed.
6. Check whether you want to prepare a first-day essentials box.
7. Select **Build checklist**, then copy the result into notes or a shared chat.

The result groups tasks into this flow:
- **Two weeks before:** confirm movers or a vehicle, sort belongings, and start boxes and labels.
- **Three days before:** empty the refrigerator, separate valuables and fragile items, and collect immediate-use supplies.
- **Move day:** check photos, meters, keys, access codes, furniture placement, and missing items.

## Empty states and errors
Choose a move date before generating a result. The planner accepts today or a future date within five years and explains whether a date is missing, invalid, in the past, or too far away. The copy button stays unavailable until a checklist exists, and a clear message appears if automatic copying fails.

## FAQ
### Is the checklist fixed?
The core structure is fixed, but highlighted tasks change based on your selections.

### Is it a replacement for a professional moving service checklist?
No. It is a practical planning draft, and you should still confirm service-specific requirements with your mover or building manager.

### Can I copy the plan into a notes app or group chat?
Yes. The result is formatted as a plain-text checklist for notes, a family chat, or a shared planning document.

### Can I use it before booking a mover?
Yes. It can help you identify the work involved and the questions to confirm before making a reservation.

### What date range can I use?
Choose today or a future date within five years. This guard prevents accidental past-date plans and implausibly long schedules.
