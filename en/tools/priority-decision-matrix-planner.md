## Why use a Priority Decision Matrix Planner?
When several tasks feel urgent at once, **deciding what to start first can waste more time than the work itself**.
This tool helps you compare urgency, impact, effort, and confidence so you can quickly sort what to do now, what to handle this week, and what to hold for later.

## How to use it
1. Add up to five tasks or options.
2. Rate urgency, impact, effort, and confidence for each one.
3. Review the ranked result with action labels like `Do now`, `This week`, or `Hold`.
4. Copy the result into your notes or task manager.

Duplicate item names are flagged instead of being ranked twice. Names support up to 80 characters and context notes up to 120 characters. Entries and results stay in your browser.

## How the priority score works
The score uses `35% urgency + 40% impact + 15% low effort + 10% confidence`. Effort is inverted, so a smaller job receives a larger quick-win contribution. This is a practical task-ranking heuristic, not an objective measure of value.

- A score of 4.1 or higher, or both urgency and impact at 4+, is labeled `Do now`.
- A score of 3 or higher, or impact at 4+, is labeled `This week`.
- The remaining items are labeled `Review later`.

Ties are resolved by impact, urgency, and then input order. Real deadlines, dependencies, staffing, safety, and legal obligations should override the suggested order.

## Good use cases
- Reordering today’s overloaded task list
- Explaining team priority choices more clearly
- Comparing which idea is worth testing first

## FAQ
### Does the top score always mean I must do it first?
No. Use it as a first-pass ranking and adjust for deadlines, dependencies, and context.

### Can I use it for team tasks?
Yes. It works for project tickets, meeting follow-ups, experiments, and personal task lists.

### Is my input sent to a server?
No. The ranking is calculated locally in your browser.
