## Adult TDEE and calorie-target calculator
Enter sex, age, height, weight, and a realistic activity level to estimate daily energy expenditure. The calculator uses the Mifflin–St Jeor equation and shows a true empty state until all required values are present.

## What it provides
- BMR estimate (Mifflin–St Jeor)
- TDEE with activity multiplier
- Daily calorie targets for cut/maintain/bulk
- Moderate and more aggressive cut/bulk comparisons
- A maintenance-calorie macro guide for protein, fat, and remaining carbohydrates

## How to use the result
1. Choose the activity level that best matches an average week without counting planned exercise twice.
2. Treat maintenance TDEE as a starting estimate, not a guaranteed requirement.
3. Compare weight trends under similar conditions for two to three weeks and adjust by 100–200 kcal if needed.

## Important limitations
This tool is for adults 18 and older. It does not account for pregnancy, breastfeeding, illness, medication, metabolic adaptation, or eating-disorder history. Its output is educational planning information and does not replace medical or dietetic advice.

## Frequently asked questions
### Should I always choose the aggressive calorie target?
No. A larger deficit or surplus is not automatically better. Start conservatively and consider professional advice if the result conflicts with health needs or symptoms.

### Why can my actual maintenance calories differ?
The activity multiplier and BMR equation are population estimates. Sleep, spontaneous movement, body composition, training load, and measurement noise can change real-world needs.
